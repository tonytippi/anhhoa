# Epic 2 Context: Thiết lập trường học và danh bộ có lịch sử

<!-- Generated from the canonical Epic 2, PassionEdu PRD, Architecture Spine, and UX contracts on 2026-09-17. -->

## Goal

Deliver the School-scoped roster foundation through which a School Admin establishes the active academic year, classes, students and their enrollment history, Parent-child authorization links, and Staff records with effective-dated class assignments. The result must make the SchoolYear a reliable boundary for later operations without rewriting prior operational facts, granting access implicitly, or allowing cross-tenant access. Epic 1 authorization, audience isolation, mutation protection, audit provenance, and tenant-isolation release proof are prerequisite gates, not behavior to recreate here.

## Stories

- 2.1: Quản lý SchoolYear và Class trong tenant
- 2.2: Tạo học sinh và enrollment có vòng đời bảo toàn lịch sử
- 2.3: Liên kết Parent pending và revoke theo từng học sinh
- 2.4: Quản lý Staff profile và phân công theo effective date
- 2.5: Chuyển lớp, chuyển năm và close-year bằng preview có đối soát

## Requirements & Constraints

- `School` is the tenant root. Every roster business query, write, uniqueness rule, audit record, and Operation is scoped to the School resolved from the active membership on every request. A route parameter, UUID, filter, header, or browser state selects context only and is never authorization evidence. Updates and deletes match both record ID and `schoolId` inside the transaction; tenant-owned relations use the composite tenant graph where supported.
- Only an authorized School Admin in an active, unsuspended School may administer this domain. Cookie-authenticated mutations require origin validation and double-submit CSRF. Audit captures School, actor identity/reference and membership where applicable, timestamp, provenance, and required reason.
- A School has at most one active `SchoolYear`. A `Class` belongs to exactly one SchoolYear and cannot be reused across SchoolYears or Schools. SchoolYear is the data boundary for later attendance and finance; do not let mutable present-state fields replace historical roster facts.
- Student codes are generated by the server, School-unique case-insensitively, immutable, and never reused. A Student has at most one enrollment in a SchoolYear. Retain Students and enrollments through lifecycle and audit rather than hard deletion.
- Enrollment lifecycle is limited to `TRIAL`, `WAITING_FOR_CLASS`, `SCHEDULED_TO_START`, `ENROLLED`, `ON_LEAVE`, `WITHDRAWN`, and `GRADUATED`. Only an enrollment that is `ENROLLED` at the relevant as-of date is eligible for later attendance and CollectionRun use; clients do not infer eligibility.
- ParentProfile is platform-global, but `StudentParent` links, queries, and audit must remain tenant-scoped. A pending link requires normalized email, full name, and phone; creating it must not create a Parent login or role. The only Parent authorization source is an active `StudentParent` link for the specific Student.
- Pending Parent binding is atomic: use normalized verified email, enforce unique Google subject and one-to-one identity binding, recheck the active link immediately before issuing a Parent session, and reject subject mismatch or reassigned email until audited revoke/reassignment. Revocation preserves history, denies the affected child/School on the next request, clears protected Parent state to a safe chooser or signed-out state, and must not remove valid links to other children or Schools.
- A Staff profile stores roster contact/demographic data only. It must not create a password, login grant, membership, role, HR/payroll record, or primary/assistant teacher classification. Keep Staff record, login/role state, binding, and assignment as separate concepts.
- Class assignments and enrollment temporal facts use the business timezone `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` and half-open intervals `[effectiveFrom, effectiveTo)`. Reject invalid or overlapping intervals. Ending an assignment or closing a year preserves readable history. `GRADUATED` is only for a child who truly leaves the school; re-enrollment requires reason and audit and never overwrites the previous enrollment.

## Technical Decisions

- The API modular monolith owns authorization, state transitions, temporal validation, generated codes, snapshots, audit, and Operations. Implement roster writes through the owning `roster` service; controllers call their owning service only and portal applications consume REST rather than API internals.
- Resolve School authorization before aggregate lookup. Explicitly verify the full School-owned graph for Student, enrollment, Class, SchoolYear, Staff, assignment, and StudentParent inside the same transaction. Global `ParentProfile` reaches a tenant only through `StudentParent`.
- Preserve source history for class transfer, year transition, and close-year. Destination enrollments/assignments are newly created only for the chosen destination; source records remain unchanged and readable. Server preview is authoritative and categorizes movable versus excluded records.
- Class transfer, year transition, and close-year batch are high-impact transactional commands. Require a client UUID `Idempotency-Key`; persist a School-scoped Operation by route, actor context, and request fingerprint; replay an identical retry and reject changed reuse. On timeout, clients must reconcile `GET /operations/:operationId` before retrying.
- Operations are authorized only to the actor context that created them. Do not introduce local optimistic lifecycle or transition outcomes. Tests must prove scoped uniqueness, cross-School graph rejection, interval validation, lifecycle retention, Parent-link revoke behavior, atomic pending binding/session recheck, and retry/concurrency safety for transition commands.

## UX & Interaction Patterns

- Use the Admin/Staff desktop-first roster workspace (`Danh bộ`) with selected School and SchoolYear visibly present in the heading or navigation. Navigation and destinations appear only when the server grants the capability. Do not surface roster actions in Ops or turn Staff records into access-management controls.
- Management lists are table-first: concise Vietnamese labels, caption, keyboard row actions, filters/sort/pagination where applicable, explicit text status, and labeled actions in the final column. On smaller screens retain identifying columns through horizontal scroll or responsive cards; never show stale rows from a previous School.
- SchoolYear/Class, Student enrollment, pending Parent link, and Staff-assignment forms show effective dates where relevant. Keep input on server validation failure, render `fieldErrors` beside the correct field, and focus an accessible error summary. Do not create local placeholder records or claim success before server confirmation.
- Distinguish profile, enrollment/history, Parent-link status, Staff assignment, and login/role state as separate surfaces. Use direct Vietnamese operational copy; keep internal IDs, raw lifecycle codes, and technical provenance out of default list rows.
- The roster transition wizard is `preview -> named confirmation -> Operation reconciliation`. Show source history, destination context, effective date, and reason. Records excluded by the server cannot be force-moved. Preserve the server preview during conflict; block confirmation until a fresh valid preview succeeds. Disable repeat submit after timeout and offer reconciliation before retry.
- Apply the shared switch guard when the user changes School with a dirty form or uncertain mutation: remain, discard only before submit, or reconcile the Operation. Never auto-save or silently change School context. Ensure focus management, keyboard dialogs, readable text statuses, and responsive behavior meet the common accessibility floor.

## Cross-Story Dependencies

- Story 2.1 establishes the SchoolYear and Class boundaries required before enrollment creation and Staff assignment.
- Story 2.2 provides Student and enrollment history required for Parent-child links and roster transitions.
- Story 2.3 extends Epic 1 Parent session admission with the active StudentParent domain and must preserve Parent cross-School/revoke safety.
- Story 2.4 supplies effective class assignment history; it does not deliver operational Teacher authorization, which later requires the separate binding, capability, teacher audience, and as-of assignment checks.
- Story 2.5 depends on the temporal roster records from the preceding stories and provides preserved as-of history consumed by later policy, attendance, finance, Parent portal, and workforce work.
