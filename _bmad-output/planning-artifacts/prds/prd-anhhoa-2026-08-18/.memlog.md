- (decision) MVP is an internal web dashboard for a kindergarten, with no parent, teacher, campus, or course management.
- (decision) Multiple Google-authenticated admins are allowlisted through ADMIN_EMAILS and invoices record both creator and payment confirmer.
- (decision) Classes, students, and bank accounts are retained through active/inactive or archived states rather than hard deletion; bank accounts can be added, activated, or deactivated only.
- (decision) Each student has at most one monthly invoice; tuition is prefilled from the current class, manual line items are supported, and invoices become read-only after payment confirmation.
- (decision) Bank-transfer invoices select an active receiving account and generate VietQR with amount and a transfer description based on student name, nickname, and class; invoice snapshots preserve historical values.
- (event) User supplied a representative tuition notice showing tuition, meals, meal adjustments, transport, overtime, leave deductions, and miscellaneous charges; invoice-template configuration per class needs analysis.
- (decision) Each class has a freely editable invoice template seeded with the school’s existing charge lines; admins can edit, reorder, add, and remove template lines, and each created invoice copies the template into immutable historical line-item snapshots.
- (change) Replaced per-class invoice templates with one school-wide editable invoice template; individual template lines use either a fixed default amount or the student class’s monthly tuition, and created invoices retain copied snapshots.
- (decision) Admins generate invoices in bulk by billing month as DRAFT, edit them, then transition them to PENDING before payment; one invoice exists per student per month.
- (decision) A PENDING invoice becomes COMPLETED only after an explicit confirmation modal; confirmation represents full payment of the invoice total, records the confirming admin and timestamp, and makes the invoice read-only.
- (decision) Completed invoices cannot be reopened or cancelled in MVP, and monthly reporting remains on-screen only with no CSV or Excel export.
- (decision) PENDING invoices lock payment content; edits require returning to DRAFT, invoice totals must be positive to progress, amounts are integer VND, and batch eligibility is evaluated at creation time with atomic duplicate prevention.
- (event) PRD finalized
